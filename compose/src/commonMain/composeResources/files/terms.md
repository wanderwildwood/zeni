## Your keys are yours

The recovery phrase is the only backup of a wallet that counts. Write it down and keep it safe. Anyone who has it can take your coins, and nobody, including the developer, can restore a wallet without it or reverse a transaction that has been sent. A Bitcoin transaction, once broadcast, cannot be undone.

Check every address and amount on your hardware wallet's own screen before you approve a payment. A tap between two phones only proposes a payment; nothing is sent until you confirm and sign.
